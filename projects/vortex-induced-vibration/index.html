2D SDOF Vortex-Induced Vibration Investigation

Tools: Ansys Fluent, MATLAB | Year: 2026

Project Overview

This project focused on analyzing the behavior of a 2D cylinder subjected to fluid flow...

Mathematical Formulation

The equation of motion for a single degree of freedom (SDOF) system can be described as:

$$ m \ddot{y} + c \dot{y} + k y = F_L(t) $$

Where $m$ is the mass, $c$ is the damping coefficient, and $k$ is the stiffness. The lift force $F_L(t)$ is extracted directly from the CFD transient simulation.

Results

Detailed analysis of the wake patterns revealed synchronization between the vortex shedding frequency and the natural frequency of the structure.
